# Individual Learning Phase: Safely Researching Online and Practically Using Emails

## Your Goal

You will practice using a web browser safely, conducting targeted research on the internet, distinguishing between secure and insecure websites, and independently writing, sending, and checking emails with attachments.

## What You Need

- A computer or laptop with internet access
- A web browser
- Access to an email account
- A text editor or note-taking program
- A small file for an email attachment, e.g., an image or a text document

## Schedule

- 10 minutes: Task 1
- 20 minutes: Task 2
- 20 minutes: Task 3
- 25 minutes: Task 4
- 20 minutes: Task 5
- 25 minutes: Extension tasks by selection or in sequence
- 10 minutes: Reflection and brief self-check

## Basic Tasks

### Task 1: Start Browser and Prepare Working Environment
**Goal:** You can open a web browser and use its basic functions safely.

**Instructions:** Open your web browser. Call up three different websites one after another, for example, a news site, a weather site, and an official government site. Use the address bar, the back button, the forward button, and the page reload function. Open at least two pages in new tabs and switch between them. Bookmark one page. Briefly note down why you used each browser function.

**Expected Outcome / Target State:** You have opened multiple websites, switched between tabs, reloaded a page, bookmarked a page, and briefly recorded your observations.

### Task 2: Simple Research with a Search Engine
**Goal:** You can use a search engine for simple and targeted research.

**Instructions:** Use a search engine to find the following information:
- Opening hours of a local civil registry office or town hall
- Weather forecast for your location for tomorrow
- Public transport timetable or connection information
Use appropriate search terms for each search. Open at least two results for each and check which result best matches your search. For each research task, note: the search term used, the website found, and why you selected it.

**Expected Outcome / Target State:** You have conducted three everyday research tasks, used meaningful search terms, and documented suitable search results.

### Task 3: Identify Secure and Insecure Websites
**Goal:** You can name and check typical characteristics of secure and potentially insecure websites.

**Instructions:** Reopen three websites from your research. For each page, check the following points:
- Does the address start with `https://`?
- Is the internet address appropriate and credible?
- Does the site appear official, current, and verifiable?
- Can you find an impressum (legal notice) or contact information?
Create a small overview with two columns: "Trustworthy" and "Suspicious." Categorize the checked pages and note at least two reasons for your assessment for each page.

**Expected Outcome / Target State:** You have evaluated multiple websites based on clear characteristics and justifiably explained why you consider them rather secure or suspicious.

### Task 4: Write and Send an Email
**Goal:** You can create and send an email with a meaningful subject and understandable content.

**Instructions:** Draft an email to a known test address or your own second account. Use a clear subject line, an appropriate salutation, and a short message text. The content should be practical, for example, an appointment request, a brief feedback, or sending information from your research. Before sending, check the recipient address, subject, and text for completeness. Send the email and then check the "Sent" folder to ensure it was successfully dispatched.

**Expected Outcome / Target State:** You have correctly created and sent a complete email, and verified its dispatch.

### Task 5: Add, Receive, and Save Email Attachment
**Goal:** You can add an attachment to an email and save a received attachment.

**Instructions:** Send a second email with a small file attached, for example, an image or a short text document. Ensure the attachment matches the content of the message. Then open the received email, download the attachment, and save it in a suitable folder. Name the storage location so you can easily find the file later. Briefly note which file you sent and where you saved it.

**Expected Outcome / Target State:** You have successfully sent an email with an attachment, received the attachment, opened it, and saved it in an organized manner.

## Extension Tasks

### Extension Task 1: Narrow Down Search Results More Precisely
**Goal:** You improve your research through more precise search terms.

**Instructions:** Take one of your research tasks from Task 2 and perform it again with more precise search terms. For example, add location, topic, or time period. Compare the new results with the first search. Document which search led to the desired result faster and why.

**Expected Outcome / Target State:** You have specifically improved a search and verifiably documented the difference between a general and a precise search query.

### Extension Task 2: Check Data Protection While Browsing
**Goal:** You observe basic precautions while browsing.

**Instructions:** Open two websites and check what personal data you would voluntarily enter there and what you would not. Also, pay attention to whether cookies, forms, or registration fields are displayed. Create a short list of at least five rules you intend to follow from now on while browsing, for example, regarding passwords, data entry, or suspicious links.

**Expected Outcome / Target State:** You have formulated concrete personal rules of conduct for safe browsing and applied them to real websites.

### Extension Task 3: Better Organize Your Email Inbox
**Goal:** You structure your email inbox clearly.

**Instructions:** Create at least two folders, for example, "Important" and "Private" or "Research" and "Attachments." Move appropriate emails into these folders. Mark an email as important or with a star, if your email program offers this function. Additionally, check spam or trash folders, without opening any links contained therein.

**Expected Outcome / Target State:** Your inbox is more clearly organized, and you have safely used basic organizational functions.

## Important Notes

- Do not open suspicious links or download unknown files.
- Pay attention to the correct internet address and `https://`.
- Only enter personal data on pages you trust.
- Always check recipient, subject, and attachment before sending emails.
- For exercises, use test messages or your own email account if possible.
- Save attachments only in folders you can easily find again.

## Reflection Questions

- Which browser functions help you most in everyday life?
- Which search terms led to the best results for you?
- How did you recognize whether a website was rather secure or suspicious?
- What will you check in the future before sending an email?
- What steps are important before opening or saving an email attachment?
- What do you want to remember permanently for safe browsing and handling emails?
