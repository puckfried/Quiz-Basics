# Individual Learning Phase: Building a Personal Study Plan and Focused Learning Environment

## Your Goal

In 2 hours, you will develop a concrete, realistic study plan for your next learning phases and set up your learning environment to enable more concentrated and efficient work. By the end, you will have a clear prioritization of your study tasks, an implementable schedule, an optimized workspace, and concrete rules against distraction.

## What You Need

- Paper or a digital note-taking tool
- Calendar or planner
- Timer or smartphone clock
- Access to your current workspace
- List of your current study tasks or topics
- Optional: Headphones, to-do app, browser with notification settings

## Time Allocation

- 10 minutes: Task 1
- 20 minutes: Task 2
- 20 minutes: Task 3
- 25 minutes: Task 4
- 15 minutes: Task 5
- 10 minutes: Task 6
- 20 minutes: Extension tasks or follow-up on open items

## Core Tasks

### Task 1:

Inventory of Your Current Learning Situation

**Goal:** You gain a clear overview of your current status, typical disruptions, and available study times.

**Work Assignment:**
Create a brief inventory with the following points:

- What study tasks or topics are currently pending?
- Which of these are urgent, which are important, and which are of lower priority?
- At what times of the day can you realistically study?
- What distracts you most frequently when studying?
- What does your current workspace look like?
- What is already working well, what is holding you back?

Note everything down in bullet points. Limit yourself to one page or screen section.

**Expected Outcome / Target State:**
You have a clear starting point with study tasks, available times, typical distractions, and initial areas for improvement.

### Task 2:

Prioritize Study Tasks

**Goal:** You logically organize your study tasks by importance and urgency.

**Work Assignment:**
Take the study tasks from Task 1 and sort them into three categories:

- work on immediately
- plan for next
- work on later

For each task, also define:

- estimated time required
- goal of the task
- whether the task requires high concentration or is more routine

Then mark the 3 most important tasks for the next 7 days.

**Expected Outcome / Target State:**
You have a prioritized task list with a clear sequence and a realistic estimate of effort and concentration requirements.

### Task 3:

Create a Personal Study Plan for 7 Days

**Goal:** You plan your study phases and breaks realistically and bindingly.

**Work Assignment:**
Create a study plan for the next 7 days. Plan for:

- fixed study blocks with specific times
- appropriate breaks
- realistic duration per study unit
- a maximum of 3 important study goals per day
- at least one buffer for unexpected events

Make sure to place demanding tasks in phases of high concentration and easier tasks in shorter or later time slots. Enter your plan into a calendar, a table, or a weekly overview.

**Expected Outcome / Target State:**
You have a complete 7-day study plan with specific times, breaks, priorities, and achievable daily goals.

### Task 4:

Optimize Learning Environment

**Goal:** You design your workspace to make concentrated learning easier.

**Work Assignment:**
Systematically check your workspace and improve it directly. Address at least these points:

- Order on your desk or desktop
- Only necessary materials laid out visibly
- Reduce or turn off notifications on devices
- Close unnecessary browser tabs and programs
- Check lighting, sitting position, and workspace
- Place water, notes, and work tools within reach

Afterward, create a short checklist "This is what my study-ready workspace looks like" with 5 to 8 points.

**Expected Outcome / Target State:**
Your workspace is visibly tidy, functionally prepared, and you have your own checklist for future study starts.

### Task 5:

Define Strategies Against Disruptions

**Goal:** You develop concrete rules to reduce distractions in your daily study routine.

**Work Assignment:**
Choose at least 4 typical sources of disturbance, for example:

- Smartphone
- Social Media
- Emails or chats
- Noise
- Multitasking
- Interruptions by others
- Lack of clarity at the start

Formulate a concrete countermeasure for each source of disturbance in the format:

- "If ..., then ..."

Example for the structure:
- If I start a study unit, then I activate focus mode for 30 minutes.

Create your personal focus rules from these.

**Expected Outcome / Target State:**
You have at least 4 concrete, applicable focus rules that you can immediately use in your study phases.

### Task 6:

Conduct First Study Unit According to Your New Plan

**Goal:** You test your study plan and new learning environment directly in practice.

**Work Assignment:**
Conduct a short 10-minute test study unit. Use:

- your prepared workspace
- a prioritized task from Task 2
- a timer
- at least one focus rule from Task 5

Afterward, briefly note:

- What worked well?
- What still bothered you?
- Was the time realistic?
- What will you adjust in your plan or workspace?

**Expected Outcome / Target State:**
You have practically tested your new approach and recorded at least 2 concrete improvements for your daily study routine.

## Extension Tasks

### Extension Task 1: Study Plan for a Whole Month

**Goal:** You transfer your 7-day plan into a longer-term, stable structure.

**Work Assignment:**
Create a rough monthly overview with:

- recurring study times
- weekly goals
- reserve times
- days with reduced workload
- planned revision phases

Mark particularly intensive weeks and balance them with lighter phases.

**Expected Outcome / Target State:**
You have a monthly overview that is realistic and combines regular study phases with recovery times.

### Extension Task 2: Secure Digital Focus Setup

**Goal:** You configure your digital tools to technically reduce distraction.

**Work Assignment:**
Adjust at least 3 settings on your device, for example:

- Deactivate notifications
- Activate focus mode or "Do Not Disturb"
- Tidy up home screen or desktop
- Set time limits for apps
- Create bookmarks or folders for study pages

Briefly document what you changed and how each setting helps you.

**Expected Outcome / Target State:**
Your most important digital distractions are reduced, and your device actively supports your learning.

### Extension Task 3: Develop a Personal Starting Routine

**Goal:** You shorten the time until concentrated work begins.

**Work Assignment:**
Develop a fixed 5-minute starting routine before each study unit. Define 4 to 6 steps, for example:

- Clear workspace
- Prepare water
- Set timer
- Note daily goal
- Turn off notifications
- Open study materials

Write down the routine in the correct order and test it once directly.

**Expected Outcome / Target State:**
You have a short, clear starting routine that you can repeat before each study unit.

## Important Notes

- Work with real study tasks from your current daily life.
- Plan a bit too sparingly rather than too idealistically.
- Set priorities clearly and comprehensibly.
- Document your results in writing.
- Adapt your plan to your real availability.
- Focus on implementable improvements rather than perfection.
- If you finish early, use the extension tasks.

## Reflection Questions

- Which study tasks truly have priority for you right now?
- How realistic is your 7-day plan?
- Which source of disturbance affects you most strongly?
- Which change to your workspace will bring you the greatest immediate benefit?
- Which focus rule will you commit to using from today onward?
- How will you know in a week that your new study plan is working?
