# 🚀 **Assignment: The Learning Methods Specialists**

### **Topic**

Development of a comprehensive collection of learning strategies for the three primary learning styles.

### **Goal of the Group Work**

Each group will become the expert team for one specific learning style. Your goal is to create a diverse and creative collection of practical learning methods for your specialty. Together, we will build a valuable pool of ideas that everyone can benefit from.

---

## **Your Missions in the Expert Teams**

### 👁️ **Group 1: Team Visual (The Sight Experts)**

> Your mission is to collect all possible learning methods that are based on **seeing**. Think of anything involving colors, shapes, images, writing, graphics, videos, and spatial arrangements.

### 🎧 **Group 2: Team Auditory (The Hearing Experts)**

> Your mission is to collect all possible learning methods that are based on **hearing**. Think of anything involving speech, sounds, music, rhythm, discussions, rhymes, and lectures.

### 🖐️ **Group 3: Team Kinesthetic (The Action Experts)**

> Your mission is to collect all possible learning methods that are based on **movement, touch, and active doing**. Think of “learning by doing,” experiments, models, and using the body.

---

## **The Final Result**

Each team creates a **digital collection of ideas** (e.g., on a presentation slide, Jamboard, or in a structured document).

**Requirements for the collection:**

- List as many methods as possible.
- Give each method a clear name.
- **Bonus:** Organize your ideas into categories, e.g.:

  - _Classics (Pen & Paper)_
  - _Digital (Apps & Tools)_
  - _Everyday & On-the-Go_

---

## **Schedule**

- **Group Work:** 20 minutes
- **Presentation per Group:** 3 minutes

  - Briefly introduce your specialty.
  - Present your **five best or most creative methods**.
