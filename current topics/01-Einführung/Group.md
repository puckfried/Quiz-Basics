# Your Task

Later, you will be taking the same specialization together. Use the next 30 minutes to get to know each other and find out what experiences, expectations, and goals each of you is bringing into the specialization.

## 1. Get to know each other

Start by taking turns. Each person briefly shares:

- Who am I?
- Why did I choose this specialization?
- What experience, interests, or knowledge do I already bring with me?

## 2. Discuss your expectations

Then talk together about the following questions:

- What would I like to learn in this specialization?
- What would I like to be able to do or understand better by the end than I can today?
- What am I particularly looking forward to?
- What might be challenging for me?
- What helps me personally when I am learning?
- What do I need or expect from the group and from the course?

You do not have to agree on everything. Different expectations and starting points are completely fine.

## 3. Prepare a short summary

At the end, agree on three points that you can share with the whole class afterwards:

- **One thing you have in common:** What connects you, or what are you all looking forward to?
- **One difference:** Where do your goals, backgrounds, or learning needs differ?
- **One question or expectation:** What would you still like to know about your specialization or the next stages of the course?

> **Important:** Make sure that everyone has roughly the same amount of space to speak and gets a chance to contribute.
