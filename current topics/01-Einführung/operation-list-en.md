# 📋 Operation List: System Exploration & Skills Check

This list is designed to guide you through the essential handling of your new hardware and operating system. Check off each task as you complete it.

---

## 📁 File Management & Handling

- [ ] **Archive Handling:** Download a `.zip` file from a trusted source and extract its contents to a specific folder.
- [ ] **PDF Interaction:** Download a `.pdf` file and open it using the system viewer or a browser.
- [ ] **Document Editing:** Download a `.docx` file, open it, and perform a "Save As" to rename the file.
- [ ] **Virtual Printing:** Open a website of your choice and use the print function to save it as a **PDF file**.
- [ ] **File Transfer:** Insert a USB stick (if available) and move one of your downloaded files onto it.

---

## ⚙️ System Customization & Tools

- [ ] **Visual Branding:** Download a picture of **Tux the Penguin** 🐧 and set it as your desktop wallpaper.
- [ ] **Calculations:** Open the built-in **Calculator** app and perform a few basic operations to test the interface.
- [ ] **Deep Dive Settings:** Navigate through the System Settings. Explore the Sound, Display, and Privacy menus.
- [ ] **Hardware Integration:** If you have access to a printer, connect it to your laptop and print a test page.

---

## 🎮 Multimedia & Documentation

- [ ] **Entertainment:** Launch a pre-installed game or a browser-based game and play for a few minutes.
- [ ] **Audio Stream:** Find an online radio station or a music streaming service and test your speakers/headphones.
- [ ] **Visual Capture:** Take a **Screenshot** of your current desktop and save it to your "Pictures" folder.

---

## 🏆 Bonus Challenges: Media Recording

- [ ] **Level 1 (Audio):** Use the built-in microphone to record a short voice memo.
- [ ] **Level 2 (Visual):** Use the webcam and microphone to record a short video greeting.
- [ ] **Level 3 (Desktop):** Use a screen recording tool to record 30 seconds of your desktop activity while explaining what you are doing (Video & Audio).
